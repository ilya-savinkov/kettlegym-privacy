KettleGym Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

In short. KettleGym uses your camera during a workout to count your repetitions. The video is analysed on your phone, in real time. It is never recorded, never stored and never sent anywhere. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and the developer receives no personal data.

About this policy

This policy describes how the KettleGym mobile app for Android and iOS ("the app", "KettleGym") handles information. KettleGym is a kettlebell training app: it shows three exercises with an animated 3D coach, counts repetitions with the front camera and times your sets and rests.

What the app processes on your device

  • Camera images. When you start a workout and allow camera access, the app reads frames from the front camera. Software that runs on your device (pose detection and a kettlebell detector) analyses each frame to find your body and the kettlebell, so the app can count repetitions and draw a skeleton over the preview. The frames are processed in memory and discarded. They are not recorded, not saved and not transmitted.
  • Workout results. When you finish a workout the app saves, in its private storage on your device, the exercise, the date and time, the duration and the repetitions and duration of each set. These are numbers only: no video, no images and no body-position data.
  • Your settings. The number of sets, repetitions and the rest time you choose for each exercise are remembered on your device.
  • Voice. Spoken cues and counts are played from audio bundled with the app, or spoken by your device's built-in text-to-speech.

Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share your personal information. In particular the app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location. It contains no advertising, no advertising identifier, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. The detection models and the exercise content are included in the app, so it does not download them.

Permissions

  • Camera (the only permission the app asks for): needed only to count repetitions during a workout. You can refuse it; the exercise guides remain available. You can withdraw it at any time in your device settings.

Where your data is stored and how to delete it

Your workout results and settings exist only on your device. To delete them, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. Because the developer holds no copy, there is nothing to delete elsewhere. Depending on your device settings, your operating system's own backup service (for example Google or iCloud backup) may include the app's data; that is controlled by you and by the platform provider, not by KettleGym.

Third-party software

The app includes open-source components that run on your device, including Google MediaPipe for pose and object detection, Android's CameraX for camera access and Filament for 3D drawing. They process data locally as described above.

Children

KettleGym is intended for adults and is not directed to children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, for example by adding an account or an online feature, this policy will be updated before the change is released and the effective date above will change. The latest version is always at this address.
